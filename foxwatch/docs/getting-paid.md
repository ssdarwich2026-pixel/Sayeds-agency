# Getting Paid — Foxwatch Revenue to Real Money

Goal: NFT sales → ETH in your wallet → USD in your bank → services stay on.
Sayed sets these up himself; nobody else touches banking or exchange accounts.

## The money path

1. Buyer purchases → ETH lands in your MetaMask (the NFT wallet, Account 4).
   Rarible takes ~2.5%; your 10% royalty applies on resales.
2. You send ETH from MetaMask → your exchange account.
3. Sell ETH → USD on the exchange.
4. Withdraw USD → your bank (ACH, 1–3 days).

## Accounts to set up (one time, ~20 min)

1. **Coinbase** (coinbase.com) — US-friendly, beginner-safe.
   - Sign up with your email, verify identity (driver's license).
   - Add your bank account under payment methods (for USD withdrawals).
   - In Coinbase, get your ETH deposit address: Assets → Ethereum → Receive.
2. **In MetaMask**, send a tiny test amount first ($5 worth), confirm it arrives,
   then move the rest. Never rush a first transfer.
3. Optional but recommended: a **separate bank sub-account or clear spreadsheet**
   for Foxwatch revenue, so sales, fees, and service costs stay visible.

## Rules

- Never share exchange passwords, API keys, or seed phrases with anyone —
  including bots, agents, and support DMs.
- Enable 2FA on the exchange (authenticator app, not SMS if possible).
- US note: crypto-to-USD sales are taxable events. Keep a simple log
  (date, amount ETH, USD value). This is not tax advice — talk to a preparer
  when revenue is real.
