## The honest math

- 53 listable artworks × 100 editions = **5,300 max edition supply at $0**, requiring 53 signatures (~90 min total signing time).
- The 5,000-token *supply* target is reachable with **zero new artwork**.
- But: unsold lazy-minted editions are listings, not on-chain tokens. They become real tokens only when bought.
- **The binding constraint is demand, not supply.** Listings do not create buyers. Every phase below is gated on sales signal, not volume.
