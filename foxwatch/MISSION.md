# Foxwatch — Mission Statement

**We build a sustainable NFT business at $0.**

Foxwatch is an open-source fox-character NFT collection. Every part of it — the
art pipeline, the metadata, the tooling — is built to run without spending money
we don't have.

## How we stay at $0: lazy minting

We list on Rarible's shared Ethereum collection with lazy minting on. Creating a
listing costs the creator nothing — no gas, no mint fees. Each NFT is minted only
when a buyer purchases, and the buyer pays the gas. Our only costs are time and
attention, and we spend those deliberately. **$0 means $0** — no spending without
Sayed's explicit approval, ever.

## What we automate, what we don't

- **Automated:** artwork validation, metadata, manifests, listing preparation,
  verification, reporting. Bots do the pencil work.
- **Never automated:** signing. Every listing is signed by Sayed's own hand, in
  his own wallet, after reviewing exactly what it authorizes. No bot will ever
  hold his private key. No signature is ever blind.
- **Never published** in his name without his exact-wording approval.

## How we scale

Supply scales through editions — tens of thousands of tokens from 53 designs.
Demand is built separately, through original fox-character content and daily
marketing. Listings don't make money; buyers do. We measure the funnel —
views → visits → sales — and follow what sells.

## How we work

Sayed owns and decides. Muse executes. ChatGPT plans. Contributors build art
and marketing through the open repo. Everything is public, tested, and
reproducible, so the project outlives any one of us and any single service.

## Why

A business that pays for itself, built in the open, that keeps its promises.
