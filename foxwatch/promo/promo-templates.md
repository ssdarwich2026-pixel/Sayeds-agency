## Reusable post templates

**T1 — Launch announcement**
> The Foxwatch opens its ranks. [N] characters. 100 editions each. First drop live on Rarible — [LINK]

**T2 — Character spotlight**
> [NAME] — [one-line description]. [PRICE] ETH. 100 editions. [LINK]

**T3 — Drop countdown**
> Drop [N] in 24h. [NAME] [one-line hook]. Will yours be next?

**T4 — Editions explainer**
> Not 1-of-1s — 100 editions per fox. Same art, lower entry. Collect the whole watch. [LINK]

**T5 — Collector shout-out**
> The watch grows. Welcome to [collector], keeper of [NAME]. [N] editions left.

**T6 — Batch drop**
> Batch [N] is live: [list 2–3 names]. [PRICE] ETH each. [LINK]

## 4-week content plan (repeats per batch)

| Week | Content | Goal |
|------|---------|------|
| 1 | Drop announcement (T1/T6) + 3 character spotlights (T2) | Launch visibility |
| 2 | Countdown (T3) + editions explainer (T4) + 2 spotlights | Educate + convert |
| 3 | Collector shout-outs (T5, even one sale) + 2 spotlights | Social proof |
| 4 | "Complete the watch" finale + next-batch teaser (T3) | Retention + repeat |

Rules: fox-character content only (original art, no IP). Every post links the Rarible
collection. Track the funnel weekly: views → profile visits → Rarible page views → sales.
No audience signal → do not increase production; fix distribution first.
