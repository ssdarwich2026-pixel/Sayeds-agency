Current Bureau Uploader

Operator: Current Bureau (channel owner, thedayfilenews@gmail.com)

What this application is

Current Bureau Uploader is a private, single-user automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Current Bureau. It is not a public service and it has no users other than the channel owner.

What it does

Its only function is to upload the owner's own original videos, together with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3. It does not access, collect, or process any other person's YouTube account or data.

Why it requests YouTube access

The application requests YouTube upload scope solely so the channel owner can publish his own original content to his own channel without manual, repetitive uploads. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Privacy

How the application handles data is described in full in its Privacy Policy.
